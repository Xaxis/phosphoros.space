# Timeline

Ten years, five lanes: proof on Earth, industry on Mercury, construction near Venus, water from Ceres,
and the work on Venus itself. Every date here is either a choice the plan makes or a date the model
derives from those choices. Move an assumption in the [Explorer](https://phosphoros.space/explore) and it
redraws this chart for your variant.

## The critical path

The critical path runs through the Foundry. The seed must launch by
[1 October 2028](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-seed-launch) so the Lattice reaches full power by
[22 July 2033](numbers.md?date=day#helios-full-power). Ice launched after
[21 October 2035](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-end) cannot arrive before the deadline, so any slip in the
doubling time comes straight out of the water.

Link by link: the seed spends [105 d](numbers.md#foundry-transfer-days) in transit and lands on
[14 January 2029](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-landing). It needs [26](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) doublings
of [60 d](numbers.md#foundry-doubling-days) to reach full scale, then [80 d](numbers.md#lattice-build-days) to
build the Lattice. The day the Lattice reaches full power the first Bricks leave Ceres, because only then is there
power to throw them. At the other end,
a Brick takes [1.15 yr](numbers.md#ice-transfer-years) to fall to Venus, and the last one must arrive by
[15 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#last-arrival), [16 d](numbers.md#ice-lowering-margin) before the
deadline. That fixes the last launch. The ice campaign is whatever time is left between the two, and the
Railheads must throw [1.0 × 10²⁰ kg](numbers.md#ice-mass) in it.

Two other links are close behind. The Veil must close before active cooling starts on
[3 May 2033](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-start): the Foundry stops growing for
[9.8 d](numbers.md#veil-diversion-days) from [7 September 2032](numbers.md?date=day#veil-build-begin) to build it,
and it closes in [December 2032](numbers.md?date=month#veil-close). The Ring must be finished before the first Brick
arrives on [16 September 2034](numbers.md?date=day#first-catch); the Foundry builds it once the Lattice is done,
and it is complete by [31 December 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ring-complete).

## Phase by phase

### Proof: 2027 and 2028

The nine experiments run in parallel from [1 January 2027](numbers.md?date=day#program-start) and must pass
by [31 December 2028](numbers.md?date=day#experiments-end) ([Research Agenda](research.md)). The cloud-life
survey flies from [June 2027](numbers.md?date=month#survey-start) and reports in
[December 2028](numbers.md?date=month#survey-end). The gate falls on
[15 December 2028](numbers.md?date=day#life-gate), after the seed has launched but before it lands: a
credible sign of native life stops the program before the Foundry touches Mercury.

### Growth: 2029 to 2033

The Foundry grows from [14 January 2029](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-landing) to
[3 May 2033](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-full-scale). Its output goes almost all into itself, because a kilogram
spent early costs all the growth it would have compounded into. The first Lattice Petals and Veil slats, from
[June 2030](numbers.md?date=month#helios-first-petals), are tests. It stops growing once, for
[9.8 d](numbers.md#veil-diversion-days) in [September 2032](numbers.md?date=month#veil-build-begin), to build the
Veil; at full scale it builds the Lattice, then from [July 2033](numbers.md?date=month#ring-build-begin) the Ring.
The atmosphere simulation has not started yet; what governs this phase is the Foundry's growth curve.

### Cooling: 2033 to 2036

Active cooling runs from [3 May 2033](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-start) to
[1 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-end), and here the day-by-day
[simulation](plan.md#the-simulated-atmosphere) takes over. Until the Ring takes over the Railheads, they
draw [6.7 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#railhead-power) of the Lattice's [8.5 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#helios-power),
and cooling is starved of power. From [16 September 2034](numbers.md?date=day#ring-pays-from) power is
plentiful and the Exhaust runs at its limit, [5.5 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#exhaust-peak). The Clear Rain begins
on [13 July 2034](numbers.md?date=day#traj-clear-rain). The seas start to freeze on
[5 July 2035](numbers.md?date=day#traj-freeze), when the air reaches the triple point, and the air holds
there until they are frozen through on [27 March 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-frozen). That leaves
[160 d](numbers.md#sealing-window) to lay the caps before the last is due on
[1 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#sealing-end): [950,000 km²/d](numbers.md#cap-laying-rate) of cap
a day. All the CO₂ is down by [12 June 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-cold) and the Frost Towers reach
their target on [28 June 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-scavenged),
[65 d](numbers.md#frost-sealing-slack) before the last caps are due
([check](numbers.md#check-frost-before-sealing)).

### Water: 2033 to 2036

Bricks leave Ceres from [22 July 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-start) to
[21 October 2035](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-end). The Ring catches the first on
[16 September 2034](numbers.md?date=day#first-catch) and the last on
[15 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#last-arrival). Lowering to the surface is planned from
[30 June 2035](numbers.md?date=day#lowering-start) to [15 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#lowering-end).
In the simulation, ice that lands while the air is below freezing stays frozen until the caps are sealed
and the air is warmed. How that ice gets down at all is still [open](risks.md#the-ring-cannot-yet-lower-the-ice).

### Breathing: 2035 and 2036

The Forges run from [1 January 2035](numbers.md?date=day#forge-start) to
[30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#forge-end), and the Aegis Shield has been on since
[15 June 2034](numbers.md?date=day#aegis-online). Once the caps are sealed the Veil opens and the simulated
air warms to [285 K](numbers.md#earth-like-temp) by [5 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-warm). It is
Earth-like on [30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-earth-like), [92 d](numbers.md#traj-slack-days)
before the deadline. The oxygen is complete on [30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#forge-end), but the
Veil waits for the Frost Towers.
Genesis seeding waits for the oceans to hold their pH as well, so it begins on
[1 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#genesis-start), and the sunlit water is green by
[12 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#genesis-end), before the deadline
([check](numbers.md#check-genesis-by-deadline)).

## What slips if the Foundry is slow

Full scale arrives at the landing date plus [26](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) doubling times. So every day
added to the doubling time pushes the Foundry's full scale, the Lattice's full power and the first ice
launch back by [26](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) days. A late seed launch does the same, day for day. What
does not move is the deadline, and with it the last useful ice launch. The slip lands in three places.

- **The water.** The ice campaign shrinks from the front, so the Railheads must throw faster to deliver
  the same [1.0 × 10²⁰ kg](numbers.md#ice-mass), or the ocean comes out shallower.
- **The energy.** The Lattice has fewer full-power years before the deadline. Every day of slip comes out of
  the [8.2%](numbers.md#budget-spare) it has to spare on its own, then out of the
  [33%](numbers.md#budget-spare-with-ring) spare the Ring's recovered energy adds.
- **The schedule.** There is little slack. A small rise in the doubling time (the
  [Budget](budget.md#how-much-room-the-plan-has) finds the edge for this and every other assumption), and the
  Frost Towers finish after the caps are due to be sealed, the oceans melt later, and the seeded life no
  longer greens them by the deadline. Under three days more per doubling and the air itself is Earth-like
  only in 2037. A much slower Foundry cannot build the Veil in time either.

To see the size of each effect, move the doubling time in the [Explorer](https://phosphoros.space/explore),
or run:

```sh
pnpm calc --set foundry-doubling-days=61     # the Frost Towers finish after the caps are due
pnpm calc --set foundry-doubling-days=63     # the air misses the deadline
```

If the Foundry cannot approach its doubling time at all, the decade is out of reach and the plan falls back
to the multi-century route ([Research Agenda](research.md#if-a-gate-fails)).
