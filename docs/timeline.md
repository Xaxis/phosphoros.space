# Timeline

Ten years, five lanes: proof on Earth, industry on Mercury, construction near Venus, water from Ceres,
and the work on Venus itself. Every date here is either a choice the plan makes or a date the model
derives from those choices. Move an assumption in the [Explorer](https://phosphoros.space/explore) and it
redraws this chart for your variant.

## The critical path

The critical path runs through the Foundry. The seed must launch by
[1 October 2028](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-seed-launch) so the Lattice reaches full power by
[9 October 2033](numbers.md?date=day#helios-full-power). The Foundry then builds the hardware for Venus, and
cooling begins when it arrives, on [23 February 2034](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-start). From there the pumps
set the pace, and any slip in the doubling time comes straight out of the
[67 d](numbers.md#frost-sealing-slack) the Frost Towers have before the last caps are due.

Link by link: the seed spends [105 d](numbers.md#foundry-transfer-days) in transit and lands on
[14 January 2029](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-landing). It needs [28](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) doublings
of [60 d](numbers.md#foundry-doubling-days) to reach full scale, a size chosen so that the Lattice and the
hardware for Venus are finished soonest: [39 d](numbers.md#lattice-build-days) to build the Lattice, then
[61 d](numbers.md#venus-hardware-build-days) for the [1.9 × 10¹⁴ kg](numbers.md#venus-hardware-mass) of
tiles, chimneys, receivers and Lanterns, more than the Foundry itself. The day the Lattice reaches full power
the first Bricks leave Ceres, because only then is there power to throw them, and the campaign lasts
[90 d](numbers.md#ice-campaign-days). The planets' places on those days set what each launch costs: Venus
leads Ceres by [214°](numbers.md#ice-launch-lead) on the first day, [7.9 d](numbers.md#ice-window-wait)
before a Hohmann window. The Bricks arrive from [13 November 2034](numbers.md?date=day#first-arrival) to
[23 January 2035](numbers.md?date=day#last-arrival), long before the pumps stop.

Other links are close behind. The Veil must close before active cooling starts: the Foundry stops growing for
[9.8 d](numbers.md#veil-diversion-days) from [7 September 2032](numbers.md?date=day#veil-build-begin) to build it,
and it closes in [December 2032](numbers.md?date=month#veil-close). And the Railheads and the Crown must be
ready for the first launch: the Ceres factories, seeded the same day as the Foundry, finish them on
[2 June 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ceres-ready), [130 d](numbers.md#ceres-slack) early, but only if they double
every [45 d](numbers.md#ceres-doubling-days) ([Ice Train](technologies/ice-train.md#ceres-factories)).

## Phase by phase

### Proof: 2027 and 2028

The nine experiments run in parallel from [1 January 2027](numbers.md?date=day#program-start) and must pass
by [31 December 2028](numbers.md?date=day#experiments-end) ([Research Agenda](research.md)). The cloud-life
survey flies from [June 2027](numbers.md?date=month#survey-start) and reports in
[December 2028](numbers.md?date=month#survey-end). The gate falls on
[15 December 2028](numbers.md?date=day#life-gate), after the seed has launched but before it lands: a
credible sign of native life stops the program before the Foundry touches Mercury.

### Growth: 2029 to 2033

The Foundry grows from [14 January 2029](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-landing) to
[31 August 2033](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-full-scale). Its output goes almost all into itself, because a kilogram
spent early costs all the growth it would have compounded into. The first Lattice Petals and Veil slats, from
[June 2030](numbers.md?date=month#helios-first-petals), are tests. It stops growing once, for
[9.8 d](numbers.md#veil-diversion-days) in [September 2032](numbers.md?date=month#veil-build-begin), to build the
Veil; at full scale it builds the Lattice, then the tiles, chimneys and receivers Venus needs, which reach
it on [23 February 2034](numbers.md?date=day#venus-hardware-ready).
On Ceres a second set of factories grows from [16 January 2030](numbers.md?date=day#ceres-landing) to
[31 March 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ceres-full-scale), then builds the Railheads and the Crown that receives the
Lattice's beams. The atmosphere simulation has not started yet; what governs this phase is the factories'
growth curves.

### Cooling: 2034 to 2036

Active cooling runs from [23 February 2034](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-start) to
[1 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-end), and here the day-by-day
[simulation](plan.md#the-simulated-atmosphere) takes over. It waits for the Exhaust's tiles, chimneys and
receivers to reach Venus, and then there is light to spare: the launches are over, and for the first
[51 d](numbers.md#traj-chimney-days) the lit canopies' chimneys, which carry
[1.8 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#chimney-capacity-lit), are the limit. The Clear Rain begins on
[8 August 2034](numbers.md?date=day#traj-clear-rain). The Bricks then undo part of it: their heat warms the
air back by [88 K](numbers.md#traj-rewarm) by [11 December 2034](numbers.md?date=day#traj-rewarm-peak),
and the rain begins for good on [30 January 2035](numbers.md?date=day#traj-rerain). The seas start to
freeze on [7 November 2035](numbers.md?date=day#traj-freeze), when the air reaches the triple point, and
the air holds there until they are frozen through on [30 April 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-frozen).
That leaves [120 d](numbers.md#sealing-window) to lay the caps before the last is due on
[1 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#sealing-end): [1.2 × 10⁶ km²/d](numbers.md#cap-laying-rate) of cap
a day. All the CO₂ is down by [17 June 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-cold) and the Frost Towers reach
their target on [26 June 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-scavenged),
[67 d](numbers.md#frost-sealing-slack) before the last caps are due
([check](numbers.md#check-frost-before-sealing)).

### Water: 2033 to 2035

Bricks leave Ceres from [9 October 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-start) to
[7 January 2034](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-end), and fall into the polar air from
[13 November 2034](numbers.md?date=day#first-arrival) to [23 January 2035](numbers.md?date=day#last-arrival).
The water they bring rains out of the cooling air on [26 November 2034](numbers.md?date=day#traj-water-rain),
gathers in the lowlands, and freezes when the air drops below freezing on
[30 June 2035](numbers.md?date=day#traj-water-frozen). It stays frozen until the caps are sealed and the air
is warmed, then melts into seas [2.17 m](numbers.md#ocean-depth) deep if spread over the globe.

### Breathing: 2035 and 2036

The Forges run from [1 April 2035](numbers.md?date=day#forge-start) to
[30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#forge-end), and the Aegis Shield has been on since
[15 June 2034](numbers.md?date=day#aegis-online). Once the caps are sealed the Veil opens and the simulated
air warms to [285 K](numbers.md#earth-like-temp) by [2 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-warm). It is
Earth-like on [30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-earth-like), [92 d](numbers.md#traj-slack-days)
before the deadline. The warmth comes first; the finish waits for the oxygen, which the Forges complete on
[30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#forge-end).
Genesis seeding waits for the seas to hold their pH as well, so it begins on
[1 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#genesis-start), and the sunlit water is green by
[12 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#genesis-end), before the deadline
([check](numbers.md#check-genesis-by-deadline)).

## What slips if the Foundry is slow

Full scale arrives at the landing date plus [28](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) doubling times. So every day
added to the doubling time pushes the Foundry's full scale, the Lattice's full power and the first ice
launch back by [28](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) days. A late seed launch does the same, day for day. What
does not move is the deadline. The slip lands in three places.

- **The water.** The campaign keeps its [90 d](numbers.md#ice-campaign-days) but starts later, and finds
  the planets elsewhere: the first launches slip past the Hohmann window and cost more, and the Bricks
  arrive later, into colder air, where their heat costs the pumps more to lift.
- **The energy.** The Lattice has fewer full-power years before the deadline, and every day of slip comes out
  of the [30%](numbers.md#budget-spare) it has to spare.
- **The schedule.** There is little slack. Two days more per doubling (the
  [Budget](budget.md#how-much-room-the-plan-has) finds the edge for this and every other assumption), and the
  Frost Towers finish after the caps are due to be sealed, the seas melt later, and the seeded life no
  longer greens them by the deadline. Four days more and the air itself is Earth-like only in 2037. A much
  slower Foundry cannot build the Veil in time either.

To see the size of each effect, move the doubling time in the [Explorer](https://phosphoros.space/explore),
or run:

```sh
pnpm calc --set foundry-doubling-days=62     # the Frost Towers finish after the caps are due
pnpm calc --set foundry-doubling-days=64     # the air misses the deadline
```

If the Foundry cannot approach its doubling time at all, the decade is out of reach and the plan falls back
to the multi-century route ([Research Agenda](research.md#if-a-gate-fails)).
