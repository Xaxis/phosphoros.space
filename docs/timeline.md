# Timeline

Ten years, five lanes: proof on Earth, industry on Mercury, construction near Venus, water from Ceres,
and the work on Venus itself. Every date here is either a choice the plan makes or a date the model
derives from those choices. Move an assumption in the [Explorer](https://phosphoros.space/explore) and the
chart moves with it.

## The critical path

The critical path runs through the Foundry. The seed must launch by
[1 October 2028](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-seed-launch) so the Lattice reaches full power by
[22 June 2033](numbers.md?date=day#helios-full-power). Ice launched after
[21 October 2035](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-end) cannot arrive before the deadline, so any slip in the
doubling time comes straight out of the water.

Link by link: the seed spends [105 d](numbers.md#foundry-transfer-days) in transit and lands on
[14 January 2029](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-landing). It needs [27](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) doublings
of [60 d](numbers.md#foundry-doubling-days) to reach full scale. The day it does, the Lattice reaches full
power and the first Bricks leave Ceres, because only then is there power to throw them. At the other end,
a Brick takes [1.15 yr](numbers.md#ice-transfer-years) to fall to Venus, and the last one must arrive by
[15 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#last-arrival), [16 d](numbers.md#ice-lowering-margin) before the
deadline. That fixes the last launch. The ice campaign is whatever time is left between the two, and the
Railheads must throw [1.0 × 10²⁰ kg](numbers.md#ice-mass) in it.

Two other links are close behind. The Veil must close before active cooling starts on
[1 January 2033](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-start): the Foundry could build it in one doubling from
[March 2032](numbers.md?date=month#veil-affordable), and it closes in
[June 2032](numbers.md?date=month#veil-close). The Ring must be finished before the first Brick arrives on
[17 August 2034](numbers.md?date=day#first-catch); it is complete by
[31 December 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ring-complete).

## Phase by phase

### Proof: 2027 and 2028

The nine experiments run in parallel from [1 January 2027](numbers.md?date=day#program-start) and must pass
by [31 December 2028](numbers.md?date=day#experiments-end) ([Research Agenda](research.md)). The cloud-life
survey flies from [June 2027](numbers.md?date=month#survey-start) and reports in
[December 2028](numbers.md?date=month#survey-end). The gate falls on
[15 December 2028](numbers.md?date=day#life-gate), after the seed has launched but before it lands: a
credible sign of native life stops the program before the Foundry touches Mercury.

### Growth: 2029 to 2033

The Foundry grows from [14 January 2029](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-landing) to
[22 June 2033](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-full-scale). Its output goes first into itself, then into the
first Lattice Petals from [June 2030](numbers.md?date=month#helios-first-petals), the Veil's slats from
[June 2030](numbers.md?date=month#veil-build-start) and the Ring from
[June 2032](numbers.md?date=month#ring-build-start). The atmosphere simulation has not started yet; what
governs this phase is the Foundry's growth curve and the dates on which it can afford each product.

### Cooling: 2033 to 2036

Active cooling runs from [1 January 2033](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-start) to
[15 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-end), and here the day-by-day
[simulation](plan.md#the-simulated-atmosphere) takes over. Until the Ring takes over the Railheads, they
draw [6.5 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#railhead-power) of the Lattice's [7.0 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#helios-power),
and cooling is starved of power. From [17 August 2034](numbers.md?date=day#ring-pays-from) power is
plentiful and the Exhaust runs at its limit, [5.0 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#exhaust-peak). The Clear Rain begins
on [7 September 2034](numbers.md?date=day#traj-clear-rain). The seas start to freeze on
[17 July 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-freeze), all the CO₂ is down by
[23 August 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-cold), the Frost Towers reach their target on
[11 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-scavenged), and the last caps are sealed by
[15 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#sealing-end).

### Water: 2033 to 2036

Bricks leave Ceres from [22 June 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-start) to
[21 October 2035](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-end). The Ring catches the first on
[17 August 2034](numbers.md?date=day#first-catch) and the last on
[15 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#last-arrival). Lowering to the surface is planned from
[30 June 2035](numbers.md?date=day#lowering-start) to [15 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#lowering-end).
In the simulation, ice that lands while the air is below freezing stays frozen until the caps are sealed
and the air is warmed. How that ice gets down at all is still [open](risks.md#the-ring-cannot-yet-lower-the-ice).

### Breathing: 2035 and 2036

The Forges run from [1 January 2035](numbers.md?date=day#forge-start) to
[30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#forge-end), and the Aegis Shield has been on since
[15 June 2034](numbers.md?date=day#aegis-online). Once the caps are sealed the Veil opens and the simulated
air warms to [285 K](numbers.md#earth-like-temp) by [19 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-warm). It is
Earth-like on [30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-earth-like), the day the oxygen is complete,
[92 d](numbers.md#traj-slack-days) before the deadline. Genesis seeding waits for the oceans to hold their pH
as well, so it begins on [14 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#genesis-start), and the sunlit water is green
by [25 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#genesis-end) ([check](numbers.md#check-genesis-by-deadline)).

## What slips if the Foundry is slow

Full scale arrives at the landing date plus [27](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) doubling times. So every day
added to the doubling time pushes the Foundry's full scale, the Lattice's full power and the first ice
launch back by [27](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) days. A late seed launch does the same, day for day. What
does not move is the deadline, and with it the last useful ice launch. The slip lands in three places.

- **The water.** The ice campaign shrinks from the front, so the Railheads must throw faster to deliver
  the same [1.0 × 10²⁰ kg](numbers.md#ice-mass), or the ocean comes out shallower.
- **The energy.** The Lattice has fewer full-power years before the deadline. On its own it already falls
  short, at [−6.3%](numbers.md#budget-spare) spare, so the plan depends on the Ring's recovered energy, and
  every day of slip comes out of the [25%](numbers.md#budget-spare-with-ring) spare that leaves.
- **The schedule.** The simulated Earth-like date at first holds, because it is set by when the Forges
  finish. A much slower Foundry cannot afford the Veil by its close date, and the simulated air misses the
  deadline.

To see the size of each effect, move the doubling time in the [Explorer](https://phosphoros.space/explore),
or run:

```sh
pnpm calc --set foundry-doubling-days=62
```

If the Foundry cannot approach its doubling time at all, the decade is out of reach and the plan falls back
to the multi-century route ([Research Agenda](research.md#if-a-gate-fails)).
