# Acid Sweep

Venus's clouds are sulfuric acid, and its lower air holds
[150 ppm](../numbers.md?show=ppm#venus-so2-fraction) of sulfur dioxide:
[1.1 × 10¹⁷ kg](../numbers.md#venus-so2-mass) of it. Cooling makes the sulfur rain out, and almost all of
the SO₂ dissolves in the CO₂ seas and freezes with them into the cryo-continents. The Acid Sweep meets the
rest, the cloud acid and the little SO₂ left in the air, with
[6 × 10¹⁵ kg](../numbers.md#acid-basalt) of crushed basalt carrying [5.9 × 10¹⁴ kg](../numbers.md#acid-cao)
of lime, so the oceans fill with fresh water rather than acid. It needs no breakthrough.

## Principle

### Where the sulfur is

The cloud deck runs from [48 km](../numbers.md?sig=2#venus-cloud-base) to
[70 km](../numbers.md?sig=2#venus-cloud-top) up. Its droplets are sulfuric acid, and they last because
of a cycle: droplets that fall below the cloud base meet air hot enough to evaporate them and break the
acid back into sulfur gases and water vapour, which rise and condense again. The clouds hold only a small
part of the sulfur. Most of it is SO₂ gas mixed through the lower air.

### What cooling does

Once the [Photonic Exhaust](exhaust.md) cools the lower air, falling droplets stop evaporating, and the
clouds rain out onto the surface: [1.0 × 10¹⁴ kg](../numbers.md#venus-cloud-acid) of sulfuric acid, from the
[0.22 kg/m²](../numbers.md#venus-cloud-column) of droplets Pioneer Venus found over every square metre
([Baines and others 2021](../sources.md#baines-2021)). That begins before the CO₂ itself condenses in the
Clear Rain of [July 2034](../numbers.md?date=month#traj-clear-rain).

### Where the SO₂ goes

SO₂ is far less volatile than CO₂, so when the CO₂ liquefies, the SO₂ goes into the liquid and runs with it
into the basins. How strongly is measured. At [263.15 K](../numbers.md#co2-so2-vle-temp), a CO₂ liquid with
[0.103](../numbers.md#co2-so2-vle-liquid) SO₂ by mole is in equilibrium with a vapour holding only
[0.0147](../numbers.md#co2-so2-vle-vapour) ([Coquelet and Arpentinier 2014](../sources.md#coquelet-2014)). That
is more SO₂ in the vapour than Raoult's law gives, because the two liquids dislike mixing a little: a
regular-solution parameter of [1.45](../numbers.md#so2-margules). Scaled as one over temperature, as a regular
solution's is, it gives a trace of SO₂ in liquid CO₂ an activity coefficient of
[5.8](../numbers.md#so2-dilute-activity) at CO₂'s triple point, where pure SO₂'s vapour pressure is
[0.072 bar](../numbers.md#so2-vapour-pressure-triple) ([Stull 1947](../sources.md#stull-1947)). Over the
seas, then, SO₂'s share of the air is only [0.058](../numbers.md#so2-partition) of its share of the liquid.

When the seas start to freeze, nearly all of the CO₂ is liquid and the air above is thin, so the seas hold
[99.4%](../numbers.md#so2-sealed-share) of the SO₂, [1.1 × 10¹⁷ kg](../numbers.md#so2-sealed), and it freezes
with them into the [cryo-continents](cryo.md), under the same caps as the CO₂. Only
[6.1 × 10¹⁴ kg](../numbers.md#so2-airborne-at-freeze) is still in the air, and most of that freezes out with
the last CO₂ frost and in the Frost Towers.

### The chemistry

Acid meets lime. Calcium oxide makes up [10%](../numbers.md#basalt-cao-fraction) of basalt, and each
molecule of it takes up one molecule of sulfur to make calcium sulfate: gypsum where there is water,
anhydrite where it is dry. SO₂ first makes calcium sulfite, which turns to sulfate once the
[Graphite Forges](forges.md) put oxygen into the air. Venus's traces of hydrogen chloride and hydrogen
fluoride end as salts in the same way.

The Sweep takes up the sulfur that is not sealed: the cloud acid and, counted in full, the SO₂ still in the
air when the seas start to freeze. That is [1.0 × 10¹⁶ mol](../numbers.md#acid-sulfur-swept), only
[0.63%](../numbers.md#acid-swept-share) of the [1.7 × 10¹⁸ mol](../numbers.md#so2-moles) of SO₂, and it takes
[5.9 × 10¹⁴ kg](../numbers.md#acid-cao) of lime, carried in [6 × 10¹⁵ kg](../numbers.md#acid-basalt) of
basalt. Version 2 sized the lime for every molecule of SO₂; the sealed sulfur needs none
([correction](../about.md#corrections)).

> **Physics:** One molecule of lime per molecule of sulfur. Every mass in this document follows from that
> ratio, the molar masses, how much sulfur Venus's air and clouds hold, and how it divides between the
> liquid CO₂ and the air.

## Design

### Rock mills

Mills in the highlands grind [6 × 10¹⁵ kg](../numbers.md#acid-basalt) of basalt to dust. Spread over the land
outside the basins, that is a layer [6.3 mm](../numbers.md#acid-basalt-layer) deep, and it uses
[4 ppm](../numbers.md#acid-crust-share) of the top kilometre of Venus's crust. Grinding matters as much as
mass: a reaction between a gas or a liquid and a solid runs at the solid's surface, and dust has vastly
more surface than bedrock.

### Dusting the land

Between the start of cooling and the Clear Rain, drones from the Exhaust canopies dust the highlands and the
future shores, so the cloud acid lands on fresh lime rather than bare rock. The basin floors, the
[33%](../numbers.md#cryo-footprint-share) of Venus where the CO₂ will pool, need none: whatever rains there is
sealed under the caps with the SO₂.

### The shore buffer

When the ice melts and the oceans form, after the caps are sealed and the Veil opens on
[2 September 2036](../numbers.md?date=day#traj-warming), crushed basalt on the shores and the sea floor keeps
the water slightly alkaline, between pH [7.5](../numbers.md#genesis-ph-min) and
[8](../numbers.md#acid-ocean-ph-max). [Genesis](genesis.md) needs it above pH
[7.5](../numbers.md#genesis-ph-min) for [90 d](../numbers.md#genesis-ph-days) before any life is released.

### What is not yet known

How fast lime takes up sulfur in the cold and dry has not been measured, and no experiment on the
[Research Agenda](../research.md) tests it. The plan does not rest on it: the sealed SO₂ needs no lime, and
the cloud acid and the SO₂ left in the air meet basalt in warm water when the oceans melt, where the same
reaction is fast.

The split itself rests on one measured temperature. CO₂ + SO₂ equilibria have been measured at
[263.15 K](../numbers.md#co2-so2-vle-temp) and above, not at the triple point, and the model carries the
measurement down with regular-solution theory. A laboratory measurement between the two is cheap and would
settle it. It is not a gate: the SO₂ left in the air would still mostly freeze out with the last frost, and
even lime for every molecule of SO₂, as version 2 planned, would take basalt from only
[0.07%](../numbers.md#acid-crust-share-all) of the crust's top kilometre.

## What is invented

> **Invented:** Nothing new in kind. The invention is scale: grinding and spreading
> [6 × 10¹⁵ kg](../numbers.md#acid-basalt) of rock across a planet before the Clear Rain.

> **Precedent:** Calcium and magnesium minerals in Venus's crust already react with its acid gases;
> Bullock and Grinspoon modeled these surface reactions as part of Venus's climate
> ([Bullock and Grinspoon 1996](../sources.md#bullock-grinspoon-1996)).

## Build sequence

- **[May 2033](../numbers.md?date=month#cooling-start):** Active cooling begins. Rock mills start
  grinding, and canopy drones start dusting the highlands and the future shores.
- **[July 2034](../numbers.md?date=month#traj-clear-rain):** The Clear Rain. By now the acid clouds
  have rained out onto dusted ground, and the SO₂ runs into the basins with the liquid CO₂.
- **[January 2035](../numbers.md?date=month#forge-start):** The Forges start, and their oxygen turns
  sulfite to sulfate.
- **[September 2036](../numbers.md?date=month#traj-warming):** The caps are sealed, the Veil opens and the
  ice melts. Shore basalt buffers the new oceans and the pH clock starts.
- **[December 2036](../numbers.md?date=month#genesis-start):** The oxygen is in and the oceans have held
  their pH. [Genesis](genesis.md) seeding begins.

## Failure modes and safeguards

- **The oceans turn acid.** The pH gate holds back every organism until the water has stayed above pH
  [7.5](../numbers.md#genesis-ph-min) for [90 d](../numbers.md#genesis-ph-days). The fix is more basalt;
  the crust has plenty.
- **A cryo-continent cell breaks open.** It releases at most
  [3.1 × 10¹⁴ kg](../numbers.md#cryo-cell-co2) of CO₂ and the sulfur frozen with it. Shore basalt and the
  oceans take up the sulfur while the cell is resealed.
- **The cold reaction is slow.** Covered above: the sealed sulfur needs no reaction, and the rest reacts in
  warm water later.
- **More SO₂ stays in the air than the model says.** The Frost Towers freeze it out with the last CO₂, and
  the shore basalt takes up what reaches the oceans. More basalt is cheap: the Sweep uses
  [4 ppm](../numbers.md#acid-crust-share) of the crust's top kilometre.

## Reproduce these numbers

The Acid Sweep's numbers are in the `acid` function of
[calc/src/model/shield.ts](../../calc/src/model/shield.ts); the SO₂ itself comes from
[calc/src/model/venus.ts](../../calc/src/model/venus.ts).

```sh
pnpm calc --only venus-so2-mass       # how much SO₂ Venus's air holds
pnpm calc --only so2-sealed-share     # the share the CO₂ seas take down with them
pnpm calc --only acid-sulfur-swept    # the sulfur left for the Sweep
pnpm calc --only acid-cao             # lime to take it up
pnpm calc --only acid-basalt-layer    # that basalt, spread over the land outside the basins
```

The [Explorer](https://phosphoros.space/explore) has no Acid Sweep slider; use `--set`. Poorer basalt
means proportionally more rock: `pnpm calc --set basalt-cao-fraction=0.05` doubles the basalt and the
layer while the lime stays the same. A more volatile SO₂ leaves more in the air:
`pnpm calc --set co2-so2-vle-vapour=0.03` doubles the measured vapour share and raises the swept sulfur.
