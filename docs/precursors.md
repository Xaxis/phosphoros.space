# Precursor Science

Phosphoros invents almost everything it builds, but its first decisions rest on facts about Venus that
nobody has measured well yet: what the deep air is made of, where the basins that will hold the CO₂ seas
lie, how hot the rock is beneath them, and whether anything lives in the clouds. Five real missions in the
plan's decade could answer parts of that. This page says what each one will tell the plan, and when.

Mission dates change often. Every date and status below was checked on 2026-11-20 against the sources
cited; where version 2 of the plan gave a different date, the table shows it.

## What each mission gives Phosphoros

### DAVINCI

NASA's probe descends through the atmosphere on 3 October 2032, the first direct measurements below the
clouds since 1985. It will measure the deep air's composition, its noble gases and the evidence for a past
ocean. For Phosphoros that is the ground truth under three technologies. The
[Photonic Exhaust](technologies/exhaust.md) and its chimneys work on that deep air. The
[Cryo-continents](technologies/cryo.md) must hold all of its CO₂. The [Acid Sweep](technologies/acid.md)
is sized from its sulfur, [150 ppm](numbers.md?show=ppm#venus-so2-fraction) of SO₂. Above all, the plan's
finish line assumes Venus's nitrogen, [3.5%](numbers.md#venus-n2-fraction) of the air, becomes
[2.1 bar](numbers.md#n2-pressure) of breathable atmosphere. DAVINCI will check that number directly.

### VERITAS

NASA's orbiter reaches Venus in mid-2033 and maps the whole surface in three dimensions, with its
composition. It tells the plan where the lowland basins are that the Clear Rain will fill, and something of
how hot the rock under them runs. The cryo-continent floors leak [12 PW](numbers.md#cryo-floor-load) at
first, an estimate that rests on basalt conducting [2.5 W/(m·K)](numbers.md#rock-conductivity), the top
of the measured range ([Robertson 1988](sources.md#basalt-thermal)). A recent study already finds
Earth-like heat flow in places on Venus, consistent with active rifting
([Smrekar 2023](sources.md#smrekar-2023-heatflow)).

### EnVision

ESA's orbiter launches in 2032 on Ariane 6, after a year's delay when NASA withdrew its radar; ESA is
studying a European radar instead ([Ars Technica](sources.md#ars-2026-envision)), though ESA's own pages
still say November 2031 ([ESA](sources.md#esa-envision)). It studies Venus as a system, from core to upper
atmosphere, including active volcanism. For Phosphoros, volcanism is a hazard: a volcano under a sealed
cryo-continent, or an unexpected supply of sulfur after the Acid Sweep, would change the design. EnVision
also informs how [Thermal Chimneys](technologies/chimneys.md) can be anchored in hot basalt.

### Venus Life Finder

Rocket Lab and MIT's privately funded probe will fall through the clouds and sample them for about five
minutes, looking for organic molecules in the droplets. It waits on Rocket Lab's Neutron rocket, whose first
launch moved to 2027 ([Space.com](sources.md#spacecom-2026-vlf),
[Rocket Lab](sources.md#rocketlab-2026-neutron)). It bears directly on the
[cloud-life gate](research.md#the-cloud-life-gate). A short descent can find evidence of life's chemistry,
but it cannot show that life is absent.

### VERVE

A small probe led by Cardiff University and proposed to ESA, it would separate from EnVision at Venus and
descend through the clouds, measuring phosphine, ammonia and other reduced gases that might signal life
([Astrobiology.com](sources.md#astrobiology-2025-verve)). We found no selection decision after July 2025.
Even if selected, it would fly with EnVision in 2032, long after the gate.

## Funding

The two NASA missions have survived one attempt to cancel them and face a second. The FY2026 budget
request proposed cancelling DAVINCI and VERITAS; Congress funded both
([Planetary Society](sources.md#planetary-2026-fy2026)), and DAVINCI's launch was brought forward to
December 2030 ([Ars Technica](sources.md#ars-2026-venus)). The FY2027 request again proposes ending both,
along with the US contribution to EnVision ([Planetary Society](sources.md#planetary-2026-fy2027)). The
Senate appropriations subcommittee's mark of 12 November 2026 directs money to both, but no FY2027 bill is
enacted, and NASA runs on a continuing resolution to 11 December 2026
([SpacePolicyOnline](sources.md#spacepolicy-2026-senate)). Earlier in 2026, reporting suggested NASA's
"decade of Venus" might shrink to a single probe ([Space.com](sources.md#spacecom-2026-decade)).

Version 2 cited a report that DAVINCI had been pushed back; its own source says the launch was brought
forward ([Mezha](sources.md#mezha-2026-venus)). Version 3 cites the original. The date changes are logged
as a [correction](about.md#corrections).

## What the precursors cannot do

None of these missions can rule out life in the clouds by the end of 2028, when Phosphoros's gate falls.
That is why the plan flies its own cloud-life survey, and why the gate is honest only if the survey's
balloons and sample return are as thorough as the [Research Agenda](research.md) demands.

There is a second gap. The data that would best inform the cooling, DAVINCI's deep air and VERITAS's maps,
arrive from late 2032 to 2034. By then the Veil has closed in [June 2032](numbers.md?date=month#veil-close)
and active cooling has begun in [January 2033](numbers.md?date=month#cooling-start). Phosphoros would start
on today's knowledge and correct course as these missions report. A slower plan would wait for them. That
is a real cost of the ten-year schedule, and the clearest argument for flying the precursors on time.
