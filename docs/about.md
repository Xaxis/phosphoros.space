# About

## What Phosphoros is

Phosphoros is a speculative but honest engineering plan. It asks what it would take to make Venus
Earth-like in ten years, from 2027 to 2036, and answers with a complete, internally consistent design:
thirteen inventions, a decade of dates, and an energy budget that shows where it closes and where it
does not. It is a design review of an
impossible-sounding idea, carried out in public.

Three rules make it honest rather than merely bold:

- **Every number is reproducible.** Each one comes from [`data/`](../data/) through the model in
  [`calc/`](../calc/), and the [ledger](numbers.md) shows its formula, its inputs and the line that computes
  it. Change an assumption and every page follows.
- **Every real-world claim cites a source**, listed in [Sources](sources.md).
- **Every invention says it is one.** Where the plan assumes engineering that does not exist, the page says
  so, and the data marks the number as invented.

Physics is the one thing it never assumes away. Every invention obeys conservation of energy and the second
law of thermodynamics, and the model checks the plan against both on every run.

## What it is not

It is not a proposal to start tomorrow, and not a prediction that anyone will. Most of its thirteen
inventions are far beyond what exists, and several may prove impossible: the [Research Agenda](research.md)
says which experiments would show that. It is not an argument that Venus should be changed. If the clouds
hold life, the plan stops, and even if they do not, the decision belongs to everyone, not to a program
([Risks and Governance](risks.md)).

What it is useful for is the map. It shows which inventions a fast terraforming would need, how good each
would have to be, and which laws no amount of invention gets around. That is worth knowing whether or not
anyone ever builds it.

## The name

Phosphoros is Greek for "light-bringer", the ancient name for Venus as the morning star. Its evening name was
Hesperos, which survives here in the [Hesperus Veil](technologies/veil.md). The name has a second meaning
for this plan: Phosphoros cools Venus by beaming its heat away into space as light.

## History

The project began as **Project Hesperus**, a conventional analysis that concluded an Earth-like Venus in ten
years was impossible without imported energy, and proposed a decade of groundwork for a transformation
lasting centuries. Its second version, **Project Hesperus Ten**, asked what would have to be invented to
finish in ten years instead, and was renamed Phosphoros. The third version, published on 2026-11-20, rebuilt
every number from first principles in code. That reproduced almost all of version 2, and found the errors
listed below.

## Corrections

### Version 3: Phosphoros, every number reproducible

Every number in the plan now comes from data/ through the model in calc/. Rebuilding the numbers from first principles reproduced almost all of version 2 and found the corrections below.

- **Correction (v3): The Veil goes back to L1, as a light-bending film.** A statite hovers only where light pushes it away from what pulls it. Sunlight travels from the Sun toward Venus, so it pushes a sunward shade toward Venus, the same way Venus's gravity pulls it: the version 2 Veil would fall onto Venus in about a day. The only place a shade can hold station between the Sun and Venus is at or just sunward of the L1 point, 1.01 million km out, where the Sun's pull balances Venus's. Version 3 puts it there and makes it a clear film that bends sunlight a few degrees so it misses Venus, rather than a mirror, so light pressure barely moves it (as in Angel's 2006 design for an Earth sunshade). The cost is area: about 3.6 times version 2's, 5.4 × 10¹¹ kg instead of 1.5 × 10¹¹ kg. The Foundry can afford that by March 2032, so the Veil closes in June 2032 instead of December 2031, still six months before active cooling. The Aegis coil moves with it, to L1, where NASA's Mars shield study put its own. ([issue #1](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/1))
- **Correction (v3): The Exhaust is sized for the peak the simulation needs.** Version 2 sized the Photonic Exhaust from the campaign's average power. The new day-by-day simulation shows the timing matters: until the Ring takes over the Railheads in August 2034 most of the Lattice's power goes to Ceres, and after that power is plentiful but the emitters cannot shine it. With 3.6 × 10¹⁹ W of emitters Venus reaches Earth-like conditions only in April 2037. With 5 × 10¹⁹ W (5.5 × 10¹³ m², 12% of Venus) the CO₂ is down by April 2036 and the air is Earth-like by 30 September 2036. The simulation also counts heat the averages left out (from the crust, the Forges and the falling ice), so the energy demand rises from 7.0 to 7.7 × 10²⁷ J: 2% spare from the Lattice alone, 28% once the Ring powers the Railheads.
- **Open problem (v3): The Ring cannot lower the ice on tethers.** Bringing [2.2 × 10¹² kg/s](numbers.md#lowering-rate) of ice to rest 300 km lower takes at least the momentum it would gain falling that far, every second: [4.7 × 10¹⁵ N](numbers.md#lowering-support). The Ring's rotor has [3.0 × 10¹³ N](numbers.md#ring-lift) of lift to spare, [150](numbers.md#lowering-overload) times too little. Options under study: drop the Bricks from rest at the Ring and pump out the extra [2.5 × 10²⁶ J](numbers.md#ring-drop-extra-heat) of heat; lower over a longer time; or a far heavier, faster rotor. The energy budget never counted the lowering's recovered energy, so it is unchanged. ([issue #2](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/2))
- **Open problem (v3): Catching east and west cancels the Ring's twist, not its braking force.** Version 2 caught half the Bricks eastbound and half westbound so the braking forces would cancel. Every Brick arrives from the same direction, and the two streams graze the Ring at mirror-image points, each about 22° forward of the line across Venus. Their sideways pushes cancel and their forward pushes add: [8.4 × 10¹⁵ N](numbers.md#ring-net-braking-force) along Venus's orbital motion, [280](numbers.md#ring-net-overload) times the rotor's spare lift. An orbital ring has no stiff way to hand that force to the planet. The catch would recover the energy the budget counts; what holds the Ring in place while it does is unsolved. ([issue #3](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/3))

The Exhaust's campaign averages are taken over the longer campaign, so they fall a little.
- **Correction (v3): Seeding waits for the ocean pH gate as well as the oxygen.** Seeding began the day the oxygen was in, [30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#forge-end), which met the pH gate (oceans above pH [7.5](numbers.md#genesis-ph-min) for [90 d](numbers.md#genesis-ph-days)) with almost nothing to spare. - **Correction (v3): The Foundry pays for what it builds.** Version 2 grew the Foundry as if the Veil and the Lattice cost it nothing, and let the Lattice's power climb with the Foundry from 2030. Mass is conserved: every kilogram of product is a kilogram the Foundry did not add to itself. Now it stops growing for [9.8 d](numbers.md#veil-diversion-days) to build the Veil, whose parts take [76 d](numbers.md#mercury-venus-transfer-days) to coast from Mercury to L1, so the Veil closes on [1 December 2032](numbers.md?date=day#veil-close), late enough that the Foundry is large. It then grows to full scale and builds the Lattice and the Ring after. Growing to about the mass of what it must build next finishes soonest, so it stops at [26](numbers.md#foundry-doublings) doublings, [6.7 × 10¹³ kg](numbers.md#foundry-final-mass), instead of 27. Active cooling starts with the Lattice's first power, [3 May 2033](numbers.md?date=day#cooling-start), instead of January 2033, and the ice launches with full power on [22 July 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ice-launch-start). The Timeline and the animation drew the Veil, the Lattice and the Ring as built from 2030 and 2032; they now follow the Foundry, with the Veil from [7 September 2032](numbers.md?date=day#veil-build-begin), the Lattice from [3 May 2033](numbers.md?date=day#foundry-full-scale) and the Ring from [22 July 2033](numbers.md?date=day#ring-build-begin).
- **Correction (v3): Cooling waits for the chimneys, and the chimneys cool the air less.** The simulation now caps the heat it removes at what the chimneys carry; the cap binds on [56 d](numbers.md#traj-chimney-days). It also charges what the chimneys cost the pumps. The exchangers cooled the air by 300 K, and the pumps see that heat over the whole range, as if the air were at the range's log-mean temperature: about 22% colder, which raised the work for each joule of the gas's heat by a third or more. Version 3's chimneys are wider, [3.5 km](numbers.md#chimney-diameter), and cool the air by [100 K](numbers.md#chimney-gas-cooling), so the pumps see [0.931](numbers.md#chimney-cold-factor) of its temperature. The bigger flow costs fan power, [1.0 × 10¹⁷ W](numbers.md#chimney-fan-power), which the budget now charges. ([issue #5](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/5))
- **Correction (v3): The Aegis coil grows to 1,000 km so it can hold itself together.** A magnet's field pulls its own winding outward. Version 2's 10 km coil would have needed about 10¹⁷ kg of the strongest carbon fibre to hold together, far more than the Foundry ever builds. The same dipole from a wider loop needs far less current, and the pull falls with the cube of the radius. At [1,000 km](numbers.md#aegis-coil-radius) the coil carries [3 × 10⁸ A](numbers.md#aegis-ampere-turns) in all, [300 A](numbers.md#aegis-current) per turn, and its structure needs at least [2.0 × 10¹¹ kg](numbers.md#aegis-structure-mass). ([issue #6](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/6))
- **Open problem (v3): The Exhaust's narrow beams cost far more pump work than the model charges.** The model charges the heat pumps the Carnot work for emitters at [2,000 K](numbers.md#exhaust-effective-temp), right for light spread over colours and directions the way a surface that hot spreads it. The tiles instead put [0.91 MW/m²](numbers.md#exhaust-flux) into a [0.4 µm](numbers.md#exhaust-band) band and a [25°](numbers.md#exhaust-cone) cone, light that counts as at least [16,000 K](numbers.md#exhaust-light-temp) by its entropy. Lifting a joule from the starting air into it takes [12](numbers.md#exhaust-work-multiple) times the work charged. Either the tiles spread their light over far more colours and directions, or the pumps need many times the power. The budget's pump work is a floor until this is solved. ([issue #4](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/4))

At 7 × 10¹⁹ W the Lattice alone fell 7% short, and the air was Earth-like only in February 2037. Version 2 raised the Lattice from 4 to 7 × 10¹⁹ W for the same reason: the launches. At [8.5 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#helios-power) the Lattice delivers [9.2 × 10²⁷ J](numbers.md#helios-supply) against [8.5 × 10²⁷ J](numbers.md#budget-demand), [7.6%](numbers.md#budget-spare) spare before the Ring's recovered energy and [33%](numbers.md#budget-spare-with-ring) after, and the plan finishes on time. - **Correction (v3): The Veil blocks every colour, and the Exhaust aims around it.** Version 2's slat was a metal mirror from 0.3 to 2.0 µm with a dielectric stack clear at the Exhaust's [2.3 µm](numbers.md#exhaust-wavelength), so the outgoing beams could pass through the Veil. The Exhaust's band holds [1.9%](numbers.md#exhaust-band-sunlight) of sunlight, and a Veil clear there would let it reach Venus: [19](numbers.md#exhaust-band-vs-veil-leak) times the [0.1%](numbers.md#veil-leak-budget) the whole closed Veil may let through. Version 3's slats block every colour, and the Exhaust aims around the Veil instead. Seen from Venus it is only [0.68°](numbers.md#veil-angular-radius) in radius, so tiles that face it tilt their cones [13°](numbers.md#exhaust-veil-tilt) off the Sun line. Gaps between slats are held to [0.05%](numbers.md#veil-gap-max), under version 2's 0.1%, so that gaps and closed slats together fit inside the budget.
- **Correction (v3): The chimneys lift heat in a liquid, not hot air.** Version 2's chimneys were ducts: deep air rose inside them, gave up its heat at the canopy and sank outside, driven by fans spaced up the column. Rising air expands and cools, and Venus's lower air already has the temperature profile of air that rose that way, so it would reach the canopy about as cold as the cloud tops, its heat turned into height. Version 3's chimneys take the heat out of the air at depth, through fan-driven intake exchangers, and lift it in sealed liquid loops, whose temperature barely changes with pressure. They work until all the CO₂ is down, [July 2036](numbers.md?date=month#traj-cold), not only until 2035, because the simulated cooling runs longer than version 2 planned. ([issue #5](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/5))
- **Correction (v3): The simulation and the flight times set the campaign's dates.** Version 2 set the campaign's later dates by hand: rain-out from June 2034, the seas freezing and the caps sealed in 2035, catching from August 2034 and lowering from August 2034, before the Clear Rain had cleared the air. Version 3 takes them from the day-by-day simulation and the Ice Train's flight times: the Clear Rain on [23 July 2034](numbers.md?date=day#traj-clear-rain), the seas freezing on [17 July 2035](numbers.md?date=day#traj-freeze), the last caps sealed by [1 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#sealing-end) and the first Brick caught on [16 September 2034](numbers.md?date=day#first-catch). Lowering is planned from [30 June 2035](numbers.md?date=day#lowering-start), and how to do it is still an open problem.

A beam keeps only [50%](numbers.md#helios-laser-efficiency) of the power through its lasers and [75%](numbers.md#helios-receiver-efficiency) through its receivers, so version 3 sends none back. The Ring's [7.5 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#ring-power) runs down the tethers to Venus's own systems, and the Helios Lattice, which already beams to Ceres, keeps doing so. The simulation counts it this way from [September 2034](numbers.md?date=month#ring-pays-from).
- **Correction (v3): The Ring needs more than eight catcher tracks.** Version 2 gave the Ring eight catcher tracks. Each Brick brakes for [13 s](numbers.md#ring-catch-time), so at the full rate [19,000](numbers.md#ring-bricks-braking) of them are on the tracks at once. Version 3 reads each from the model. The Chimneys and the Exhaust start with active cooling, [2033](numbers.md?date=year#cooling-start); the Lattice counts at full power, [2033](numbers.md?date=year#helios-full-power); the Veil when it closes, [2032](numbers.md?date=year#veil-close); the Ring at its first catch, [2034](numbers.md?date=year#first-catch); the Forges when they start, [2035](numbers.md?date=year#forge-start); the Cryo-continents with the Clear Rain, [2034](numbers.md?date=year#traj-clear-rain); and Choir with the landing, [2029](numbers.md?date=year#foundry-landing).

- **Correction (v3): The seas freeze at the triple point, and the simulation's heat books balance.** The simulation released all of the CO₂'s latent heat evenly between its critical point and its triple point, although [5.7%](numbers.md#co2-triple-vapour-share) of the CO₂ is still vapour at the triple point and liquid CO₂ cannot freeze above it. Now the CO₂ gives up its heat of condensation as it rains out along the vapour curve; at the triple point the seas give up their heat of fusion, [210 kJ/kg](numbers.md#co2-fusion-heat), while the air holds at [216.6 K](numbers.md#co2-triple-temp); and the CO₂ still in the air gives up the whole deposition heat as it freezes out below. The air reaches the triple point on [17 July 2035](numbers.md?date=day#traj-freeze), the seas are frozen through on [15 April 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-frozen), all the CO₂ is down on [3 July 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-cold), the Frost Towers finish on [19 July 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-scavenged) and the air is Earth-like on [30 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-earth-like). The Frost Towers also counted the leftover CO₂'s latent heat a second time, the pumps ran on past the end of the cooling path, and heat entering after the pumps stopped was counted as lifted. With those fixed, the heat lifted equals Venus's heat, less the [1.6 × 10²¹ J](numbers.md#traj-heat-left-airborne) the CO₂ left in the air still holds, plus the heat added while the pumps run, and a new check keeps it so. The time to lay the caps, [140 d](numbers.md#sealing-window), and the rate of laying them, [1.1 × 10⁶ km²/d](numbers.md#cap-laying-rate), are now model numbers. ([issue #10](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/10))
- **Correction (v3): Unrefrigerated floor heat melts the base of the ice.** The model priced the floor heat, if it is not pumped away, with the deposition heat, as if it turned the ice straight to gas. Under [2 km](numbers.md#cryo-thickness) of dry ice the base is at [280 bar](numbers.md#cryo-base-pressure), far above the triple-point pressure, so the heat melts it, which takes only the heat of fusion, [210 kJ/kg](numbers.md#co2-fusion-heat): [0.4%](numbers.md#cryo-unrefrigerated-loss) of the CO₂ a year at first, [7.6 m](numbers.md#cryo-base-melt-rate) off the base of the sheet. The liquid is lighter than the ice above it and carries its weight, so it lubricates the sheet and presses toward the cell walls. The basal refrigeration the plan already carries keeps the base below its melting point, [222 K](numbers.md#cryo-base-melt-temp). ([issue #10](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/10))
- **Correction (v3): The Exhaust grows a tenth so the seas freeze before the caps are sealed.** Releasing the heat of fusion at the triple point holds the air at [216.6 K](numbers.md#co2-triple-temp) while the seas freeze, with the Exhaust at its peak and the falling ice adding most of the heat that enters meanwhile. At the old peak the seas froze only in June 2036, the Frost Towers finished after the last caps were due on [1 September 2036](numbers.md?date=day#sealing-end), and seeded life greened the oceans after the deadline. The peak grows to [5.5 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#exhaust-peak): the seas are frozen through on [15 April 2036](numbers.md?date=day#traj-frozen), the Frost Towers finish [44 d](numbers.md#frost-sealing-slack) before the last caps, and the oceans are green on [12 December 2036](numbers.md?date=day#genesis-end). It costs little energy, because a faster freeze lets less heat in: the Lattice still has [7.6%](numbers.md#budget-spare) to spare on its own. ([issue #10](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/10))
- **Correction (v3): The SO₂ freezes into the cryo-continents, so the Acid Sweep takes up only what is left.** Version 2 sized the lime to neutralize every molecule of Venus's SO₂. But SO₂ is far less volatile than CO₂, and measured CO₂ + SO₂ equilibria put it in the liquid when the CO₂ condenses. When the seas start to freeze at the triple point, they hold [99.4%](numbers.md#so2-sealed-share) of it, and it freezes with them under the caps. The lime falls to [5.9 × 10¹⁴ kg](numbers.md#acid-cao) and the basalt to [6 × 10¹⁵ kg](numbers.md#acid-basalt), spread [6.3 mm](numbers.md#acid-basalt-layer) deep on the land outside the basins. ([issue #8](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/8))
- **Correction (v3): The longest beam to Ceres starts from the Lattice, not the Ring.** Version 2 measured the longest beam to Ceres from Venus, because the Ring beamed the Railheads' power there. Version 3 sends nothing back from the Ring; the Lattice beams Ceres itself, from orbits as far out as [0.45 AU](numbers.md#helios-orbit-outer). The real limit is heat: the receivers and the Railheads make [70](numbers.md#ceres-heat-vs-surface) times more than Ceres's whole surface could radiate at [1,500 K](numbers.md#helios-fin-temp), so almost none of it can be made on the ground. - **Finding (v3): Ceres's new orbit stays clear of Jupiter; resonances are not modelled.** Ceres's new aphelion stays [1.66 AU](numbers.md#ceres-jupiter-gap) inside Jupiter's perihelion, [4.7](numbers.md#ceres-jupiter-gap-hill) of Jupiter's Hill radii, and its Tisserand parameter with respect to Jupiter falls from [3.31](numbers.md#ceres-tisserand-now) to [3.21](numbers.md#ceres-tisserand), still above 3. - **Correction (v3): The Foundry's target is what it builds next.** The Foundry's target mass was typed in as 6.0 × 10¹³ kg, about the Lattice and the Ring before the Lattice grew. - **Open problem (v3): The receivers' and the Ring's loss heat falls on Venus.** Version 3 said the receivers' loss heat, [2.8 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#helios-receiver-loss), and the heat the catcher tracks shed, [1.9 × 10¹⁹ W](numbers.md#ring-catch-loss-power), radiate to space and never reach Venus. ([issue #14](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/14))
- **Open problem (v3): The Ring cannot carry the receivers the plan hangs on it.** The stator's mass, [3.0 × 10¹² kg](numbers.md#ring-stator-mass), was said to include the Lattice's receivers. - **Open problem (v3): Nothing builds the hardware at Venus in time.** The Foundry's books counted the Foundry, the Veil, the Lattice and the Ring, but not the Exhaust's tiles and canopies ([3.0 × 10¹³ kg](numbers.md#exhaust-tile-mass)), the chimneys ([1.4 × 10¹³ kg](numbers.md#chimney-mass)), the receivers and their fins, or the Lanterns. ([issue #18](https://github.com/Xaxis/phosphoros.space/issues/18))
